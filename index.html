What does SPECS stand for?

SPECS (Students Pursuing Education in Computer Science) is a student-led club at The Masters School that encourages a passion for technology and computer science. The club offers resources, opportunities, and support for students interested in learning more about the field. SPECS aims to create a community where members can explore, grow, and deepen their knowledge of computer science in a supportive and engaging environment.
